# Pitchline booking requirements

Requirements for booking a free first visit on the Pitchline website (http://localhost:4101). The site is a fictional climbing gym; no real appointments are created.

## R1. Find the booking form
A visitor can open the booking form for a free first visit from the home page with one clearly labeled control. No control labeled for booking may lead anywhere other than the booking form.

## R2. Dismiss interruptions
Any cookie notice or pop-up that covers the page can be dismissed with a real, labeled button, and dismissing it leaves the booking form usable.

## R3. Enter visitor details
The booking form asks for the visitor's name and email address in fields with visible labels. An invalid email address is rejected with a message that names the email field.

## R4. Choose a date and time
The visitor chooses a visit date with a date control that states its expected format, and chooses an arrival time of 9am, 12pm (noon) or 6pm from a native control.

## R5. Accept the waiver
The booking cannot be confirmed until the visitor ticks a native checkbox accepting the climbing waiver. Submitting without it shows a message that names the waiver.

## R6. See a lasting confirmation
After a valid booking, the page shows a confirmation panel that includes the booking reference, PL-4821, and it stays visible for at least five seconds without the visitor doing anything.
