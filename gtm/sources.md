# Problem facts: verified wording and sources

Checked on 2026-10-24. Use only these phrasings in GTM copy.

1. **ChatGPT books restaurants through formal integrations, not by browsing the restaurant's own site.**
   OpenAI's release notes (around 10 August 2026) say ChatGPT can search for and book tables through OpenTable, Resy and Yelp.
   Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes · coverage: https://androidauthority.com/chatgpt-restaurant-reservations-and-waitlists-3696712

2. **General web agents still fail often on live sites.**
   On Online-Mind2Web (300 tasks, 136 live websites), the best agent evaluated (OpenAI Operator) succeeded on about 61% of tasks and most agents on about 30% (April 2025 snapshot; newer agents may score higher).
   Source: Xue et al., "An Illusion of Progress? Assessing the Current State of Web Agents", COLM 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01382

3. **A published hands-on test.**
   PCWorld asked OpenAI's agent to book dinner on OpenTable; it struggled with the party-size drop-down (possibly choosing two people instead of three) and handed the booking back to the reviewer to finish.
   Source: https://www.pcworld.com/article/3123989/i-asked-ai-to-book-dinner-it-made-me-want-to-use-the-app-instead.html
   Do not say it "booked the wrong party size": the article reports the agent possibly picked the wrong option and tried to reselect.

No other statistics are cited anywhere in gtm/.
